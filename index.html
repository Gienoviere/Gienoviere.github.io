About Me

I am an 26 old South American man who is striving to be game designer/Software engineer A jack of all trade master of both, I have been at it for 8 years and am currently studying to become Game designer or Software engineer and want to get bachelor diploma and maybe master as well. After that I want to work at a game studio or software studio before opening my own company. Growing up in 90s, I have enjoyed many different gaming console with each their games, I have played on Xbox Original/PS2/GameCube/Gameboy (advance)/PSP/ Xbox 360 with Kinect/ Nintendo DS Lite/WII/ Xbox One with Kinect/PS4/Xbox Series. As you can read, I have been with nearly every generation of consoles and quite enjoyed a lot of genres games as RPG. So, I have a broad sense of how games are made and constructed. I am learning C#/C++ as programming language for Unity/Unreal Engine 5, Which I also am learning to work with to become full-fledged game designer. For game designer position, I am using Blender to learn design landscape/terrains/characters. I also am thinking to learn Autodesk Maya. Though you can use UE5 or unity tools to design stuff too but with blender or Maya I have more control over what I want to visualize.
